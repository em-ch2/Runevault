Runevault · sealed in trust

Sealing the deal.

Every Runevault transfer is sealed twice before it sets sail: once by your own hand on a physical stone, and once by a keeper of lore who reads the seal and judges the voyage. Only then does the vault open and the money leave port.

How a deal is sealed

  1. Chart your courseChoose the destination and the amount. Your request waits in the harbour, pending.
  2. Press the sealSix glyphs appear. Press them in order on your Pico; it signs the approval on the device itself.
  3. Set sailGemini reads your seal and checks the voyage for danger. If the waters are safe, the bank sends the money.

The glyphs and their lore

Every seal is made of three ancient glyphs. Once your transfer clears, the keeper of lore writes a short tale from the glyphs you pressed, so no two seals tell quite the same story.
